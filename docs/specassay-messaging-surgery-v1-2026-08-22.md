## 0. The strategic ruling (record with its reasoning)

The page currently sells a DISCIPLINE (traceability). The cold-landing CTO hears discipline as overhead, and historically he was right: hand-maintained matrices, for auditors, read by no one. The page now sells a MOMENT: the briefing that arrives on the pull request at the instant someone is about to sign a change to what the system is supposed to do. "What currently answers for the sentence you are changing" is the question every reviewer has silently wanted answered, and in 2026, when agents generate diffs faster than humans build mental models, that anxiety is acute and funded. Painkiller, not medicine. The discipline is how the moment gets manufactured; it moves below the fold, for the already-convinced.
