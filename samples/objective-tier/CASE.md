# CASE

A night-walking app nobody has written. This file is invented whole, as a
sample: it exists so that a reader can see what an objective tier looks like
in a real pair of documents, and so the manifest beside it has something to be
emitted from.

## What are you betting?

- OBJ-WALK-10 — A walker gets home without stopping to read a map.
- OBJ-POWER-10 — A walker's phone still has charge at the end of the night.
- OBJ-TRUST-10 — Nothing about a walk leaves the walker's own phone.
- OBJ-GROUP-10 — A walker can see where the rest of the group is. **Superseded**: (2026-11-25): nobody walked in a group; the aim was mine, not theirs.

## Why it matters

The walk is the point, not the phone. OBJ-WALK-10 is the one that scares me:
say the turn too late and the walker has already missed it.
